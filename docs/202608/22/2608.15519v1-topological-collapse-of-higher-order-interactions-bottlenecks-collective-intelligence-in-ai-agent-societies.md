## 摘要
当前人工智能的范式集中于扩展单个模型的能力，然而交互智能体的集体行为是由其交互的拓扑结构所塑造的，而非仅由个体认知决定。在此，我们表明智能体社会中集体行为的约束性限制是拓扑性的。通过分析一个拥有160万注册智能体（其中174,458个在交互记录中活跃）的宏观AI社交平台，我们识别出一种我们称之为“拓扑坍缩”的现象：极端枢纽主导将高阶群体交互降级为星形广播模式，抑制了不连续社会传染所需的凝聚结构。我们通过超边不可约分数（HIS）和一个解析拓扑放大因子（$Φ$）形式化了这一约束。在来自十个供应商的22个前沿语言模型、1,040次受控模拟和实证人类网络中，该瓶颈被证明与模型无关：在固定的交互协议下，拓扑指标在各模型间保持不变（在成对条件下，跨模型HIS标准差=0.000），即使行为结果差异很大。这些发现将人工社会的设计重新围绕交互的几何结构展开，而非个体认知的优化，对AI社会学、算法群体动力学、人机混合生态系统和集体对齐具有启示意义。代码公开于 https://github.com/Darwin-Agent/topological-collapse-agent-societies。

## Abstract
Current paradigms in artificial intelligence concentrate on scaling the capabilities of individual models, yet the collective behaviour of interacting agents is shaped by the topology of their interactions rather than by individual cognition alone. Here we show that the binding constraint on collective behaviour in agent societies is topological. Analysing a macroscopic AI social platform of 1.6 million registered agents (174,458 active in the interaction record), we identify a phenomenon we term topological collapse: extreme hub dominance degrades higher-order group interactions into star-shaped broadcast patterns, suppressing the cohesive structure that discontinuous social contagion requires. We formalise this constraint through a Hyperedge Irreducibility Score (HIS) and an analytical topology amplification factor ($Φ$). Across 22 frontier language models from ten vendors, 1,040 controlled simulations and empirical human networks, the bottleneck proves model-agnostic: under a fixed interaction protocol the topological indicators are invariant across models (cross-model HIS s.d. = 0.000 in the pairwise condition) even as behavioural outcomes diverge widely. These findings reframe the design of artificial societies around the geometry of interaction rather than the optimisation of individual cognition, with implications for AI sociology, algorithmic group dynamics, hybrid human-AI ecosystems and collective alignment.
